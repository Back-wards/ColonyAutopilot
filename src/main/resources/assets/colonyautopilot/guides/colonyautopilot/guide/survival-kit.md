# Survival Kit — Cures, Immunity & Counter-Tech

Everything Spore gives *you* against the infection (all of it real, registered
Spore items — these DO show in JEI, unlike the player-head items).

## Immunity: the Gas Mask

The single best piece of anti-infection gear in the game. A **gas mask** — or
any of Spore's flesh helmets (**Living Helmet**, **Living Helmet MK2**, **Flesh
Helmet**), or the Inquisition's own **CCM** cryo mask, which is a gas mask
underneath (its **SCM** upgrade is a diamond helmet, not a mask: it does NOT
grant immunity, so keep the CCM for creep runs) — worn on your head makes the
Mycelium debuff
**bounce off entirely** — every infected attack, cloud, burst and block that
would infect you is cancelled at the source. Zero armor, unbreakable, total
immunity. Put one on before every expedition into creep.

## Cure: the Milky Sack (and plain milk)

Already infected? Eat a **Milky Sack** — it strips *every* harmful effect
(Mycelium, Madness, Corrosion and friends). An ordinary **milk bucket** also
clears the debuff. The one hard rule: **cure the living.** Whatever dies while
infected converts — curing has to happen before the killing blow, not after.

## Cold — the hard counter

Every infected creature, mini-boss and calamity is **freeze-weak**. Cold is
not a gimmick here, it is the doctrine:

* **Snowballs actually work** — each hit stacks real frozen ticks on any
  infected target. A snow farm is a munitions plant.
* **Cryo Grenades** ([recipe](cleansing-nukes.md)) — a real, JEI-visible item
  you **throw** (right-click). Within ~25 blocks it turns fungal blocks to
  Freezerburned Biomass, blanks spore foliage and freezes anything infected —
  the same ~50-block-wide blast the old Ice Bomb had.
* The **Cryogenic Aspect** enchantment adds heavy freeze on hit; the frost
  **PCI** weapon multiplies freeze damage against the horde.
* Freezing a mound before the kill keeps it from alerting the Proto
  ([bestiary](bestiary.md)).

## The CDU — Cryogenic Dispensing Unit

Spore's terrain-cleansing **machine**: fuel it and it works a large area
(~50 blocks across) around itself, continuously —

* **freezes the creep's own blocks** — mycelium block, fungal shell, overgrown
  spawners, crusted bile, rooted mycelium and organite all turn to
  Freezerburned Biomass (it no longer restores infested stone or dirt to clean
  terrain — for that you need the colony's creep guard or an SZC),
* **removes creep growth** and freezes remains,
* **deletes tendrils and scent trackers outright** — for a long time the only
  thing in the game that could; the cryo chestplates now do it too (**CCC**,
  the Cryo-Combat Chestplate, clears every tendril within 16 blocks; **SCC**,
  the SubZero-Combat Chestplate, clears tendrils and scents) just by being worn,
* stacks **frostbite** on every infected in range, and dusts the area with snow.

One CDU at the edge of your base rolls the front line back while you sleep.
It pairs perfectly with the colony's own creep guard: the guard cleans colony
chunks, the CDU holds anywhere you plant it.

## The flesh arsenal

Spore's own weapons are grown, not forged — and fed, not reloaded:

* **Four biological guns** — the Acidic Assassin, Bile Blaster, Toxic
  Terroriser and the **Mycelial MistMaker** (a shotgun/chainsaw hybrid that
  reloads on the flesh of its enemies: right-click to deploy the saw,
  left-click to fire). They hit hard but wear out fast — each carries only
  15–60 durability, so carry spares. You feed them food and biomass; the vials
  for ammo are harvested from the infected.
* **The Frostwerfer** — the cold gun, and the one thing here that isn't flesh.
  Craft it from a CDU, a PCI, a circuit board and six Reinforced Compound
  Plates; hold right-click to spray a frost stream, shift-right-click with ice
  canisters to refuel. Its recipe is handed to you automatically.
* **A full infected melee line** — knives to greatswords to scythes, many with
  armor-piercing minimum damage or kill-reward effects.
* **TACZ firearms and Ars Nouveau magic** (this pack's additions) sit happily
  alongside all of it.

## ⚠ Most syringes are NOT medicine

Spore's plain agents — the **Refining Agent**, **Hardening Agent** and
**Integrating Agent**, plus the raw **Syringe** and the **Mutating Syringe** —
look medical, but they are the **gear-upgrade system**, used to mutate and
strengthen the flesh weapons and armor. Several of them **inject Mycelium into
whatever they touch**, including you. Upgrade with them; never "treat" anyone
with them.

**The five named ones are the exception.** Spore Inquisition's *surgery table*
brews **Valetudo**, **Corpulentia**, **Pertinacia**, **Celeritas** and
**Natatio** from organs plus the matching reagent (they are re-labelled Blood
Samples, so look for the name, not for the word "syringe"). Hold one and use it
on yourself: it raises your base health, damage, armour, speed or swim speed
**permanently**. They are the only injections meant for a human being.
