# Getting Started

On your first join of a world you receive the **founder's satchel**:

* a **Town Hall** block (the world's founder only — if a village already stands
  you get a pointer to it instead) and the **build tool** — place the Town Hall
  where the village should grow; everything else follows from it
* the **Zone Marker** wand — mark ground the autopilot must never build on
  (see [Protected Zones](protected-zones.md))
* this **Field Guide**
* a blank **Akashic Tome** plus the pack's other manuals — the **AE2 Guide**,
  Ars Nouveau's **Worn Notebook** and the **Mystical Agriculture Guide Book** —
  sneak-right-click any guide book onto the tome to bind it (binding is manual by design; a pre-bound tome refuses to morph).
  Mods with no manual of their own are covered in [The Other Mods](mod-primer.md)

## Your first days

1. **Place the Town Hall.** The autopilot places, builds and upgrades every other
   building on its own, following a growth plan — three builders' huts first, then
   tavern, warehouse, blacksmith, farms, guard towers, university, barracks and the rest.
   (That is the order of a new growth-plan file; one an older version wrote keeps its
   own order.)
2. **Don't fight the machine.** Citizens are hired, fed, healed, equipped and
   researched automatically. Materials for construction trickle in on their own.
3. **Mark your ground.** If you build a base near the colony, surround it with
   Zone Marker zones early — the autopilot refuses to build inside them.
4. **Mind the calendar.** The world starts at peace. After the grace period
   (**about 50 game-days**, set at world creation, counted down in chat every
   5 days), the **RedNight** falls once — and the infection begins. The colony
   races toward population 25 — its survival bar for the war — and its combat
   research on its own, so spend the time gearing yourself (gas mask, cold,
   guns) and scouting.
5. **The other monsters keep a calendar too.** With Born in Chaos and
   Mutationcraft installed, their monsters do not spawn naturally until their
   day comes. By default that is **day 25** for Born in Chaos and the first
   mutants, **day 50** for advanced mutants, **day 100** for elite mutants and
   **day 200** for the mutant bosses; your server's arrivals settings may
   differ. The chat counts down the first arrival for its last 25 days; the
   later mutants come without a warning.

## While you wait

Explore. The world out there has dungeons, structure bosses and loot — and the
colony grows fine without you. Night mobs **will** kill the odd citizen; that is
the intended pressure, and the colony replaces its losses.
