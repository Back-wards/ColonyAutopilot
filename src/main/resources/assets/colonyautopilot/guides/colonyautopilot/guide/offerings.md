# Offerings & the Finale

In this pack the apocalypse **never ends on its own** — corruption caps at 99%
and waits for you. The finale is player-triggered by feeding the RedNight a
**three-course meal** of escalating offerings. Each offering **raises**
corruption and summons an escort of calamity bosses — risk and reward climb
together.

**The ritual:** drop the offering (default Q) onto the giant mound's **biomass
floor** near a fully-grown mound.

The RedNight's gifts in return carry no ColonyBucks.

## The Finale

At **99% corruption, any one more offering** begins the finale: after the lore
plays out (~90 seconds) you are teleported into the arena dimension. **It starts
empty.** Four Guardians stand between you and the end — Abundantia (Famine),
Vis (Feebleness), Spes (Despair) and Amor (Hatred). The first wakes within 20
minutes and the chat names the spot ("You can feel a strange presence in
0/500"). Kill a Guardian and it drops a glowing **eye key**: **right-click the
eye**, or nothing else happens — using it is what wakes the next Guardian. Use
the fourth eye (Amor) and the RedNight's tower rises, with the **"Nunny"** — the
RedNight's own Proto — inside. **Kill it and reach the exit** — win, and the
infection ends. Budget a long session, and expect the game to hang for half a
minute or more when the tower and its mountain appear: let it finish.

Read [the bestiary](bestiary.md) before you go: the Proto caps every hit you
land at 20 damage and vomits mini-bosses when struck. Bring sustain, not one
big sword.

Impatient? The finale and corruption can also be driven directly by
[command](commands.md).
