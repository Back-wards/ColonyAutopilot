# Fighting Back

## What the colony fields on its own

* **Guards** — knights and gunners (archers where gunners are switched off, marksmen
  once researched) garrison the barracks and up to 20 standalone
  guard towers; the towers upgrade in lockstep with the Town Hall. One **druid**
  per four fighters heals and debuffs, kept stocked with potion reagents. In
  Progression Mode the war pays: a buck from a guard's kill goes straight into the
  treasury, and each raider that falls in a raid pays one more (up to the raid's size).
* **Iron golems** — two patrol the Town Hall and one guards every blacksmith; a
  fallen golem is reforged a game day after it falls. Their corruption chance is turned way
  down (10%), so the infection rarely steals them. Don't want them? Switch **Village
  golem** off on the town hall's Autopilot page (or an operator runs
  `/colonyautopilot golems off`): the forging stops and the standing ones leave
  (never `/kill` a golem near the creep — a golem that *dies* can rise as a construct).
* **K-Turrets** — the autopilot makes every turret and drone **invulnerable** and
  teaches them to target the infection's whole horde (without it they'd ignore
  spore creatures entirely). A turret is a true static defence — remove it only
  through its own menu.
* **The creep guard** — colony chunks are scrubbed of creep down to 48 blocks below
  the surface, infested earth is cured (podzol, coarse and rooted dirt the creep
  took come back as plain dirt), the ground the creep ate is filled back up to
  where the patrol last found it clean (no crater, no flooded pit), and the four
  spreaders (mound, gastgeber, tendril, scamper) are killed on sight inside the
  colony. Bosses are spared, and so is the RedNight's own primordial mound.
  Nothing inside a protected zone is scrubbed: the creep guard leaves a zone
  alone (its spreaders are still killed).
* **Building repair** — a hut the creep chews holes into has its missing blocks
  re-laid from its blueprint, no builder needed (a building under construction is
  left to its builders, and so are the towers of a barracks being built, even while a
  stalled order of it is filed again); stocked racks and anything else
  standing there (your chest, a block you changed) are left as they are. A hut the infection
  fully destroys rises again, even after a restart and after the creep guard has
  cleaned the ground around it (your own Spore lamps and lab blocks beside it are
  left alone, a building you deconstruct stays down, and a hut you pull down
  yourself stays down unless the infection itself stands beside it). Where the creep
  is at work, the ground it hollowed out under a building is filled back (never
  under a mine; a cellar you dig under a hut wants a protected zone), and the
  roads it ate are laid again. Ordinary blocks are
  always repaired free; in Progression Mode (while the exchange is on) a repair or
  a rebuild pays for the exchange's goods it pastes (gold, diamond and netherite
  blocks and their kin), infection or not, and one the treasury cannot pay for
  stays out of the re-paste. In Progression Mode a repair restores walls, not
  contents: a missing chest comes back empty, an item frame without its item.
* **Citizen care** — the infection cannot kill a citizen or visitor, it only
  slows them; the spore's other effects are cured for a credit (in Progression
  Mode paid from the treasury, waiting while it is empty); the janitor purges
  spore drops and mob junk from every rack and pocket so the war never silts the
  warehouse (the pack ships this on — `purgeMobDrops` in the config; it is off
  for the standalone mod).

## What you bring

* **A gas mask on your head and milk in your bag** — total infection immunity
  and the cure; the full [Survival Kit](survival-kit.md) covers both.
* **Kill the spreaders** — every mound you kill and tendril you clear outside the colony is
  ground the colony never has to fight on. **Freeze mounds before the kill** —
  an un-frozen kill of a mature mound alerts the Proto ([bestiary](bestiary.md)).
* **An Eye of the Vigil in the base chest** — dropped by Vigils, and the *only*
  thing that removes a **Challenge Watcher** squatting in town: right-click it
  away. Nothing else touches it, `/kill` included ([bestiary](bestiary.md)).
* **Break the watch-towers** — the fungal ruins hide an **Outpost Watcher**, an
  invisible block wearing an animated eye that showers everyone within ~80
  blocks with Scents, Umarmers and Usurpers. Guards and turrets can't fight a
  block — but one good axe can, and the eye is on Spore's removable-foliage
  list, so three other things clear it for free: a pair of **Cryo-Combat
  Boots** (within 6 blocks, while you wear them), any **CDU** in range, and a
  thrown **Cryo Grenade**. If citizens keep dying to "nothing" near a ruin,
  walk the ruin in the boots, plant a CDU, or find the eye and pop it
  ([bestiary](bestiary.md)).
* **Cold** — snowballs, cryo grenades, freeze enchants and the **CDU** machine; the
  whole horde is freeze-weak ([Survival Kit](survival-kit.md)).
* **Guns** — TACZ firearms (automate ammo with Create) and Spore's own
  biomass-fed flesh guns.
* **Magic** — Ars Nouveau spells, wards and summons.
* **Machines** — Mekanism suits and turbines for the endgame.
* **The counter-weapons** — the craftable [Cleansing Nukes](cleansing-nukes.md),
  the only way to push corruption back.

## The one rule

Night mobs attacking citizens is **intended** — the colony is built to take losses
and replace them. Population 25+ with the combat research done survives the
RedNight; a colony of 20 tends to fall. Buy the colony time and it wins.
