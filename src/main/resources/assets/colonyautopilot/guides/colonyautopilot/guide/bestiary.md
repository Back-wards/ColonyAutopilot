# Bestiary — Know Your Enemy

Every creature on this page comes from Spore and Spore Inquisition and appears
only where they are installed. They are listed from the ground up, in
ascending order of threat. The names are the ones the game shows; where a
command needs a different id, it follows in brackets.

## The spreaders

Kill these: they ARE the infection
([how creep spreads](the-infection.md#how-creep-actually-spreads)).

* **Mound** (the game's *Flesh Mound*): the terrain-eater. It grows through
  four stages, its creep ring widening from radius 10 to 25 with Spore
  Inquisition (stock Spore: 5 to 20), and it breeds tendrils from stage 3.
  With Spore Inquisition a fully grown mound doesn't sit forever: every 25
  minutes the hive bursts it into a buffed Scamper, which bursts into fresh
  mounds a few minutes later unless you kill it first. **Two fight rules:**
  (1) it puffs a defensive **Mycelium II cloud when you hit it** (at most once
  every 15 seconds, and the cloud hangs that long), so melee bathes you in
  infection: bring a gas mask or shoot it.
  (2) **A mature, linked mound ALERTS the nearest Proto as it dies** and can
  call a calamity onto your head, **unless frost was the last thing to hurt
  it**. Frosting it is not enough by itself: snowballs, a thrown
  [Cryo Grenade](cleansing-nukes.md) or a **snow golem** standing within 16
  blocks only start the frost, which hurts the mound every two seconds once it
  is frozen through. So finish it with the **Frostwerfer**; or stop hitting,
  wait for a frost tick and land ONE last blow within two seconds; or let the
  frost kill it. In a cold biome or in powder snow every kill is silent, and
  that includes land an SZC or a Frozen Kiss has frozen.
* **Tendril**: a small planter, **invulnerable** to every weapon. It carries
  the infection forward and turns corpses and chests of food into new mounds,
  and monster spawners into Infested Spawners.
  Three things remove it: the [CDU](survival-kit.md) machine deletes it;
  wearing a cryo chestplate erases every tendril within 16 blocks while you
  stand there (the iron **CCC**, Cryo-Combat, or the diamond **SCC**,
  SubZero-Combat); and inside your colony the creep guard removes it.
* **Gastgeber** (gastgaber): a tall walker infecting the ground it crosses.
  An ordinary kill; just do it early.
* **Scamper**: a walking seed. Left alive a few minutes, it bursts where it
  stands into two or three new mounds (now and then into a Gastgeber instead).
  Kill it on sight, wherever it is: one you kill seeds nothing. The RedNight
  and every bursting mound send out buffed ones.
* **Scent**: the infection's homing beacon, a hanging puff of tan spore-dust.
  Scents are dropped on you in battle, planted by fleeing Vigils and sprayed
  over your citizens by Outpost Watchers (below). A Scent is not just a
  marker: **while it lives it summons infected onto its spot** and feeds
  biomass to any nearby Womb. Scents fade on their own after a while; the CDU
  clears them at once, and the SubZero chestplate (**SCC**) deletes every
  Scent within 16 blocks just by being worn. A patch of ground with lasting tan
  particles and endless "random" mobs almost always has a Scent (or its
  planter) on it.

*(Inside your colony the creep guard kills mounds, Gastgebers, tendrils and
Scampers on its own, the RedNight excepted, so this section is for your
expeditions.)*

## The support organoids

Spawner-type creatures that keep the horde coming. All are ordinary kills: put
them first in a defence.

* **Vigil**: a tall watcher that **runs away** when you close in and burrows
  out of reach ("*You are feeling watched...*" in chat means one has eyes on
  you). Every escape escalates: first it drops a Scent, then an overcharged
  Scent, then it reports your position straight to the Proto. Catch it before
  it dives, and always loot it: **Vigils drop The Eye of the Vigil**, the one
  tool that removes a Challenge Watcher outright (below).
* **Verwahrung** (verva): a living spawn-pod. About **four seconds** after it
  digs out it hatches a speed-and-strength-buffed fighter; kill the pod before
  it opens and the fighter never exists.
* **Usurper**: burrowing artillery. It cannot be hurt while digging out, and
  it wanders off underground after a minute with nothing to shoot at.
* **Umarmer** (umarmed): a grappler that seizes you, rides you and pumps
  Mycelium into your face. **Melee it**: its shielding quarters all projectile
  damage, so guns are the worst possible answer.
* **Braurei**: a flesh-witch with buff auras for the horde and splash debuffs
  for you. It cannot be hurt while it is still digging out, so be ready and
  kill it the moment it is up, before its auras spread.
* **Womb** (reconstructor): assimilates infected corpses into biomass (5 each)
  and banks one more every 30 seconds by itself. It **builds a full calamity
  boss at 100 banked**. Kill it before it banks about 50, which an idle Womb
  reaches in roughly 25 minutes; past that, even its *death* births a
  weakened, burning calamity.

## The Challenge Watcher

The arena director (arena_tendril): a flesh stalk delivered by the
[ordeals](the-infection.md#protos-and-the-ordeals) (every DUSK wave carries
one, up to three at high corruption) and by the RedNight itself, which raises
one beside it for every blow you land on it. In the finale the tower's Proto
raises **four at once** every 45 seconds while anyone stands within 30 blocks
of it.

It never attacks. It *judges*: it weighs the armour and health of
**everything alive within 16 blocks, you, your citizens, your golems, all of
it**, banks a wave budget from the total, then every 15 seconds digs
Verwahrung pods and flesh bombs out of the ground around itself, tolling a
bell per wave. And one nasty side rule: anything hovering **more than 3 blocks
above it** gets free Usurpers spawned at it, outside the budget. So don't
fight it from the air, and know that citizens on walls and rooftops feed it
the same way.

**You cannot kill it. At all.** Weapons, arrows, magic, even an operator's
`/kill` bounce off. Your two honest outs:

* **Right-click it with The Eye of the Vigil**: instant removal. Vigils and
  Outpost Watchers drop the Eye.
* **Starve it**: kill its pods and spawns and stay off its airspace. Once the
  budget is spent and its brood is dead it burrows away on its own, and even
  drops loot for your trouble.

Inside a colony the starving route can stall forever (the town never stops
feeding it targets), so keep an Eye of the Vigil in the base chest like a fire
extinguisher.

## The Outpost Watcher

The infection's watch-tower. Not a creature but **a block**: a lidless eye
grown into Spore's ruined structures (the Mega Biomass Tower and the Prison),
and easily the most confusing thing in the plague, because the block itself
renders invisible. All you see is the floating, animated eye.

Spore Inquisition grows many more, and some can land right beside your town:

* Every flesh spire an ordeal raises carries one. The MIDNIGHT wave raises
  three to nine spires, 30 to 125 blocks around the player nearest the world
  spawn, and from 70% corruption the DAWN wave raises one too.
* At its MIDNIGHT a Proto raises a tall flesh column on its own spot, and about
  45 minutes later fifteen of them sit up its height.
* From 75% corruption the RedNight raises a huge flesh tower over the world
  spawn every hour, with five in it.

It sleeps until something living enters its range: **a ±80-block cube with
Spore Inquisition** (±50 with Spore alone), a whole town quarter. Then, every
10 seconds, it looks at every player, citizen, golem and animal in range and
rolls for *each of them*: a 30% chance to plant a **Scent** on them (tan puffs
that keep calling infected onto that spot while they last) and a 10% chance to
spawn an **Umarmer or Usurper directly on top of them**, a fresh roll every
time. A colony inside its range is a conveyor belt of victims: your animals
that die infected come back as **Evoker Claws**, and your citizens, who never
convert, simply die. It ignores creative-mode players entirely, which is why
an admin can stand in the carnage seeing nothing wrong.

**The fix is beautifully mundane: walk up and break the block.** It is a
little harder than an iron block and it's axe-mineable: a good axe pops it in
a couple of seconds, every roll stops with it, and it always drops **The Eye of
the Vigil** (keep that for the next Challenge Watcher). Your guards can never
do this for you: to them it's terrain. A few other things clear it too, but
only breaking it yourself is sure to give you the Eye:

* the iron **CCB** boots (Cryo-Combat) erase it within 6 blocks while you wear
  them, the diamond **SCB** (SubZero-Combat) within 8;
* a **CDU** in range, or a thrown [Cryo Grenade](cleansing-nukes.md);
* a **snow golem** parked right beside it.

If citizens keep dying to "nothing" near a fungal ruin or where an ordeal
landed, go find the eye.

## Bioblob

A lump of fused flesh (biobloob; 50 health) that hits hard up close and spits
from range. It **grows with every kill**, up to twice its size with health and
damage to match, and **shrinks as you hurt it**, getting faster as it gets
smaller. The NOON ordeal digs up two (four from 17.5% corruption), and from
60% every Proto adds seven every 10 minutes. Kill them while they are small.

## The Hypers

Eleven elite infected, the mini-bosses: **Wendigo, Inquisitor, Groberfub**
(grober), **Hexenmeister** (hevoker), **Brotkatze** (brot), **Ogre,
Axtwerfer** (hvindicator), **Hollenhund** (hollen), and the **Reaper**, the
**Specter** and the **Vanguard** (below). They arrive with ordeal waves and
hatch from the pods *the Proto plants when you hit it*, and from 20%
corruption every Proto sends out five of them every 20 minutes. Tanky and
hard-hitting, but killable by a geared player or a guard squad with druid
support.

Three of them need notes of their own:

* **Reaper** (80 health): fights up close and spits from range, and it eats:
  it strips or rots crops and saplings within 32 blocks and feeds on what it
  kills. Once fed, it **feeds the infected around it**, handing them one by
  one the kill they need to evolve. A crowd of plain infected near a Reaper
  turns into Knights and Griefers without killing anything
  ([how they grow](the-infection.md#the-infected-grow)). Kill it first. The
  DUSK ordeal always brings one.
* **Specter** (70 health): a spy that turns **invisible** when it picks a
  target more than about 7 blocks away, and raids chests and barrels for food.
  DUSK brings one from 52.5% corruption.
* **Vanguard** (110 health): a crossbow hyper that marches on the nearest
  vanilla villagers or village (it does not look for your colony) with a
  raiding party of infected illagers and evolved infected, loading chunks on
  its way. DUSK brings one from 17.5% corruption.

## The Calamities

The nine siege bosses: **Sieger, GazenBreacher, Grakensenker** (kraken),
**Stahlmorder** (stahl), **Hindenburg, Howitzer, Verfalldrache**
(verfall, a dragon), **Hohlfresser** (a burrowing worm) and **Leviathan** (a
multipart titan). These are what the ECLIPSE raid (a GazenBreacher, a
Hindenburg and a Sieger for each target), the [offerings](offerings.md) and a
full Womb summon. They ignore i-frames with some attacks and force-load their
own chunks. They are all **freeze-weak**: cold is your opener. Heavy armour
alone will not save you: at least a fifth of every hit they land goes straight
through it, and most of an overcharged one's.

With the autopilot's default settings they **break no blocks and blast no
craters**, though their hits still land in full. See
[Walls that hold](fighting-back.md#walls-that-hold) in Fighting Back.

## Hive Tumor

The hive's heart in the field (the game calls it *Hivetumor*): a pulsing
mini-boss that is a hive in miniature. In Spore Inquisition 5 the DUSK ordeal
digs one up at 35% corruption and a second at 70%, and in the finale the
tower's Proto raises one every minute while you stand within 45 blocks of it.

* **Every 20 seconds it gives every infected creature within 100 blocks
  Symbiosis** (a Spore buff), and **every hour while somebody is online it
  adds a step of world corruption** (a Proto adds one every half hour).
* While it has a target, it raises mounds, Umarmers and Usurpers under that
  target's feet.
* It no longer sends out waves of its own (4.5: it sent a full spread of
  evolved infected every 10 minutes and five hypers every 20), and with Spore
  Inquisition it never grows into a Proto.

Rule of thumb: the bosses are the point of the endgame, but a Hive Tumor next
to your colony is a now-problem: everything infected around it fights harder,
and the corruption keeps climbing while it lives.

## Proto

The hive brain, the true boss (the game calls it *Proto Hivemind*). Low health
on paper, but:

* **Every hit you land is capped at 20 damage**: a god-sword swings for 20
  here. Only a single absurd hit (10,000+) bypasses the cap. Plan for
  **sustained** damage, not burst.
* **Small hits make it call mini-bosses.** A hit of 20 or less (most swords
  and guns are under the cap) has a one-in-five chance, at most once every 8
  seconds, of making it plant one to three Verwahrung pods around itself, each
  hatching a hyper. A hit above 20 is cut to 20 and draws no answer: a few
  heavy blows are the quiet way, automatic fire is what calls the adds. Clear
  the pods or drown.
* It heals 1 health every second unless it is frozen, and gains damage
  resistance below half health. In the finale arena it also regenerates at
  full speed while any Hive Tumor lives within 200 blocks of it, so kill every
  Hive Tumor it raises before you go back to the Proto.
* Knockback-immune, immune to acid and to weak potion effects.
* **Cold is its soft spot**: while it is frozen it stops healing, and the cryo
  gear's frostbite eats at it as at every infected. The finale's Proto is the
  exception: it shakes frostbite off at once. (Spore also tries to give a
  frozen Proto Weakness, but it shrugs off weak harmful effects, so that never
  sticks and it keeps its resistance below half health.) A Proto hurt by frost
  sometimes panic-spreads creep 50 blocks around itself.
* It directs the calamities and force-loads a huge area around itself: while a
  Proto lives, its front advances even with nobody online. It also turns the
  land around it into the Wastes
  ([Protos and the ordeals](the-infection.md#protos-and-the-ordeals)).
* **Its own spawns grow with world corruption** (Spore Inquisition 5). Every
  10 minutes one of each of fourteen evolved kinds, two of each from 80 points
  (20%), three from 160 (40%), and seven Bioblobs on top from 240 (60%). Every
  20 minutes, from 80 points, one each of five hypers (Inquisitor, Brotkatze,
  Wendigo, Ogre, Hollenhund), two each from 160, and a Reaper, a Specter and a
  Vanguard from 240. It also sends out Gastgebers: about twenty an hour from
  the start, over thirty an hour from 80 points. All of them are flung far out
  from the Proto, up to 175 blocks (some Gastgebers up to 200). (4.5: three of
  each evolved kind and two of each hyper from the start.)
* **An ordinary Proto has a lifespan.** It runs DAWN, NOON, DUSK and MIDNIGHT
  off one countdown and then **kills itself about 53 minutes of loaded time
  after MIDNIGHT**, leaving the world for the next Proto. Don't bank on one
  particular Proto: check it is still alive before you commit.

**The colony spares Proto and Hive Tumor on purpose**: they are the endgame,
and the finale needs a Proto to kill. Fight it on your terms in the
[finale arena](offerings.md), not in your wheat field.
