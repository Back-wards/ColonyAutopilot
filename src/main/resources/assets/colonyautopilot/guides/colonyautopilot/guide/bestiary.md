# Bestiary — Know Your Enemy

The infection's forces in ascending order of "run".

## The spreaders (kill these — they ARE the infection)

* **Mound** — the terrain-eater. Ages four times, ring radius 15→30 in this
  pack, breeds tendrils from age 3. **Two fight rules:** (1) it puffs a
  defensive **Mycelium II cloud every time you hit it** — melee bathes you in
  infection, so bring a gas mask or shoot it; (2) **a mature, linked mound
  killed while un-frozen ALERTS the nearest Proto** and can call a calamity
  onto your head. **Freeze it first — a frozen kill is a silent kill.**
  Snowballs, the **Cryo Grenade** (thrown, not dropped), the **Frostwerfer**
  and cold weapons all count — and a **snow golem freezes every infected
  within 16 blocks of it** just by standing there.
* **Tendril** — invulnerable to weapons. The [CDU](survival-kit.md) deletes
  it, and so does simply wearing one of the cryo chestplates — the iron
  **CCC** (Cryo) or the diamond **SCC** (Sub Zero): either erases every
  Tendril within 16 blocks while you stand there. Inside the colony, the creep
  guard removes it. Turns corpses and loot into new mounds.
* **GastGeber** — a tall walker infecting the ground it crosses. An ordinary
  kill; just do it early.
* **Scamper** — suicide-seeder; becomes a mound where it dies. Kill it OUT
  there, not at your gate.
* **Scents** — the infection's homing beacons: hanging puffs of tan spore-dust
  dropped on you in battle, planted by fleeing Vigils, and sprayed over your
  citizens by **Outpost Watchers** (further down this page).
  A Scent is not just a marker — **while it lives it summons infected onto its
  spot** and feeds biomass to any nearby Reconstructor. They fade on their own
  after a while; the CDU clears them instantly, and the Sub Zero chestplate
  (**SCC**) deletes every Scent within 16 blocks just by being worn. A patch of ground with
  persistent tan particles and endless "random" mobs almost always has a Scent
  (or its planter) on it.

*(The colony's creep guard kills mounds, gastgebers, tendrils and scampers inside
colony chunks automatically — the RedNight's own mound excepted — so this section
is for your own expeditions.)*

## The support organoids

Spawner-type creatures that keep the horde coming. All ordinary kills —
prioritize them in a defense:

* **Vigil** — a tall watcher that **runs away** when you close in and burrows
  out of reach ("*You are feeling watched...*" in chat means one has eyes on
  you). Every escape escalates: first it drops a Scent, then an overcharged
  Scent, then it reports your position straight to the Proto. Catch it before
  it dives — and always loot it: **Vigils drop The Eye of the Vigil**, the only
  tool that removes a Challenge Watcher (below).
* **Verva** — a living spawn-pod. About **four seconds** after it digs out it
  hatches a speed-and-strength-buffed fighter; kill the pod before it opens
  and the fighter never exists.
* **Usurper** — burrowing artillery. It cannot be hurt while digging out, and
  it wanders off underground after a minute with nothing to shoot at.
* **Umarmer** — a grappler that seizes you, rides you and pumps Mycelium into
  your face. **Melee it** — its shielding quarters all projectile damage, so
  guns are the worst possible answer.
* **Braurei** — a flesh-witch: buff auras for the horde, splash debuffs for
  you. Interrupt it early; it can't fight back while emerging.
* **Reconstructor ("the Womb")** — assimilates infected corpses into biomass
  and **builds a full calamity boss at 100 banked**. Kill it before it banks
  ~50 — past that, even its *death* births a weakened, burning calamity.

## The Challenge Watcher (the arena director)

A skinny three-block flesh stalk delivered by the [ordeals](the-infection.md)
— the DUSK wave always carries one — and by the RedNight's primordial mound,
which answers anyone flying within ~30 blocks with **four at once**. Sightsee
that thing from a distance.

It never attacks. It *judges*: it weighs the armor and health of **everything
alive within 16 blocks — you, your citizens, your golems, all of it** — banks
a wave budget from the total, then every 15 seconds digs Verva pods and flesh
bombs out of the ground around itself, tolling a bell per wave. And one nasty
side rule: anything hovering **more than 3 blocks above it** gets free
Usurpers spawned at it, forever, outside the budget — so don't fight it from
the air, and know that citizens on walls and rooftops feed it the same way.

**You cannot kill it. At all.** Weapons, arrows, magic, even an operator's
`/kill` bounce off. Your two honest outs:

* **Right-click it with The Eye of the Vigil** — instant removal.
* **Starve it** — kill its pods and spawns and stay off its airspace; once the
  budget is spent and its brood is dead it burrows away on its own, and even
  drops loot for your trouble.

Inside a colony the starving route can stall forever — the town never stops
feeding it targets — so keep an Eye of the Vigil in the base chest like a
fire extinguisher.

## The Outpost Watcher (the infection's watch-tower)

Not a creature — **a block**: a lidless eye grown into Spore's ruined
structures (the Mega Biomass Tower and the Prison), and easily the most
confusing thing in the pack, because the block itself renders invisible —
all you see is the floating, animated eye.

It sleeps until something living enters its range — **a ±80-block cube in
this pack**, a whole town quarter. Then, every 10 seconds, it looks at every
player, citizen, golem and animal in range and rolls for *each of them*: a
30% chance to plant a **Scent** on them (tan particle puffs that keep calling
infected onto that spot while they last) and a 10% chance to spawn
an **Umarmer or Usurper directly on top of them**, a fresh coin flip every
time. Anything of yours that dies out there comes back as a **Claw**. A colony inside its range is a conveyor belt of victims. It ignores
creative-mode players entirely, which is why an admin can stand in the
carnage seeing nothing wrong.

**The fix is beautifully mundane: walk up and break the block.** It is a
little harder than an iron block and it's axe-mineable — a good axe pops it in
a couple of seconds, and every roll stops with it. A CDU sweep and a thrown
Cryo Grenade also remove it. Your guards and turrets can never do this for
you: to them it's terrain — but a **snow golem** parked beside one will break it.
If citizens keep dying to "nothing" near a fungal ruin, go find the eye.

## The Hypers (mini-bosses)

Ten elite infected — **Wendigo, Inquisitor, Grober, Hevoker, Brot, Ogre,
H-Vindicator, Hollen**, plus the **Vanguard** and the **Reaper** that the DUSK
ordeal brings. They arrive with ordeal waves and are *vomited out by
the Proto when you hit it*. Tanky, hard-hitting, but killable by a geared
player or a guard squad with druid support.

## The Calamities (the nine siege bosses)

**Sieger, Gazenbreacher, Kraken, Stahlmörder, Hindenburg, Howitzer,
Verfalldrachen** (a dragon), **Hohlfresser** (a burrowing worm),
**Leviathan** (a multipart titan). These are what the ECLIPSE raid and the
[offerings](offerings.md) summon. They break blocks, ignore i-frames with some
attacks, and force-load their own chunks. They are all **freeze-weak** — cold
is your opener. Heavy armor alone will not save you: several of their attacks
set a guaranteed minimum damage that pierces protection.

## Hive Tumor — the wave generator

A pulsing mini-boss that is a hive in miniature: **every 8 minutes it pushes
out a full spread of evolved infected, and every 20 minutes five hypers**
(Inquisitor, Brot, Wendigo, Ogre, Hollen), scattering them up to 75 blocks
from itself. It will not grow into a Proto in this pack. This pack's rule of
thumb: the bosses are the point of the endgame — but a Hive Tumor next to your
colony is a now-problem: it just keeps shipping waves at you.

## Proto — the hive brain (TRUE BOSS)

The centerpiece. Low health on paper, but:

* **Every hit you land is capped at 20 damage** — a god-sword swings for 20
  here. Only a single absurd hit (10,000+) bypasses the cap. Plan for
  **sustained** damage, not burst.
* **Hitting it makes it vomit mini-bosses** — roughly one retaliation summon
  every few hits, delivering Hypers to your position. Clear the adds or drown.
* It **self-heals** constantly and gains damage resistance below half health.
* Knockback-immune, immune to acid and to weak potion effects.
* **Cold is its soft spot** — freeze effects stack Weakness on it (though a
  frozen Proto occasionally panic-spreads creep).
* It directs the calamities and force-loads a huge area around itself: while a
  Proto lives, its front advances even with nobody online.
* **An ordinary Proto has a lifespan.** It runs DAWN, NOON, DUSK and MIDNIGHT
  off one countdown and then **kills itself about 53 minutes of loaded time
  after MIDNIGHT**, leaving the world for the next Proto. Don't bank on one
  particular Proto — check it is still alive before you commit.

**The colony spares Proto and Hive Tumor on purpose** — they are the endgame,
and the finale needs a Proto to kill. Fight it on your terms in the
[finale arena](offerings.md), not in your wheat field.
