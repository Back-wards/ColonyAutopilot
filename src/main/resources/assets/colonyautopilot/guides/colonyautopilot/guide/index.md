# Colony Autopilot Field Guide

*Colony Autopilot, by Backwards.*

Place the **Town Hall** and walk away — the village builds itself. Where
**Fungal Infection: Spore**, **Spore Inquisition** and **Mutationcraft** are installed,
this guide also covers the plague that wants to eat it.

Use the **sidebar** to browse chapters, or the **search bar** at the top to find
anything — items, recipes, commands.

## Chapters

### Your colony

* [Getting Started](getting-started.md) — the founder's satchel, picking your style,
  your first days, playing together, what to do when something looks stuck, and
  updating from an older version
* [ColonyBucks](colonybucks.md) — Progression Mode: what the colony pays for, how you
  earn the bucks, and how to switch it off
* [Autopilot Systems](autopilot-features.md) — everything the autopilot runs for you,
  what it does while you are away, and your colony's own settings
* [Protected Zones](protected-zones.md) — keep the autopilot off your own builds
* [Fighting Back](fighting-back.md) — the colony's defences and yours: guards, the
  militia, golems, walls that hold, and guns

### The plague (where installed)

* [The Spore Infection](the-infection.md) — how the plague spreads and grows, the grace
  period, the RedNight and the corruption clock
* [Bestiary](bestiary.md) — every spreader, mini-boss and calamity, with fight notes
* [Survival Kit](survival-kit.md) — gas mask, cures, the cold doctrine, the CDU
* [Cleansing Nukes](cleansing-nukes.md) — the craftable nukes and the Cryo Grenade
* [Offerings & the Finale](offerings.md) — how the apocalypse ends, and what the
  finale costs the town: read it before the last offering
* [The Mutants](mutants.md) — Mutationcraft: when they come, the sickness, how to
  fight them

**With Spore Inquisition:** the offering heads and the three cleansing nukes are
renamed player heads, so JEI's item list and the creative search do not show them.
Craft them from the recipes in this guide; an operator can also hand out the offerings
by [command](commands.md). The Cryo Grenade and the Frostwerfer are ordinary items: JEI
lists them under Spore Inquisition.
