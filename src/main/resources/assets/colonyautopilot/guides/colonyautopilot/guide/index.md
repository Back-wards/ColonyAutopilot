# Colony Autopilot Field Guide

*Colony Autopilot, by Backwards.*

Place the **Town Hall** and walk away — the village builds itself. This guide covers
the two halves of the pack: the colony that runs itself, and the fungal apocalypse
that wants to eat it.

Use the **sidebar** to browse chapters, or the **search bar** at the top to find
anything — items, recipes, commands.

## Chapters

* [Getting Started](getting-started.md) — the founder's satchel and your first days
* [The Spore Infection](the-infection.md) — how the plague actually works
* [Bestiary](bestiary.md) — every spreader, mini-boss and calamity, with fight notes
* [Fighting Back](fighting-back.md) — the colony's defenses and yours
* [Survival Kit](survival-kit.md) — gas mask, cures, the cold doctrine, the CDU
* [Cleansing Nukes](cleansing-nukes.md) — the craftable counter-weapons

* [Commands](commands.md) — click-to-run command reference
* [Protected Zones](protected-zones.md) — keep the autopilot off your builds
* [The Other Mods](mod-primer.md) — quick-starts for every mod without a manual
* [Autopilot Systems](autopilot-features.md) — everything the addon runs for you

**Important:** most Spore items in this guide (the offering heads and the two
cleansing nukes) are renamed vanilla player-heads driven by datapack functions. They
will **never appear in JEI or creative search** — craft them with the recipes shown
here, or use the [commands](commands.md). The Cryo Grenade and the Frostwerfer are
the exception: real items, in JEI under Spore Inquisition.
